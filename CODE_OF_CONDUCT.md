# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a good project:

- Giving and accepting technical criticism about the work, not the person
- Saying "I was wrong about this" when the evidence says so
- Explaining the *why* behind a decision, including what it cost

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an
individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer via the email on
[@ernestgonzalezv](https://github.com/ernestgonzalezv). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by the project's leadership.
